Warehouse geometry and textures
Source: https://github.com/wh200720041/warehouse_simulation_toolkit
Source commit: 18df8ce6dde61711e0705f3bd872465a01bd01a2
License: BSD-3-Clause; see licenses/WAREHOUSE-BSD-3-Clause.txt.
Warehouse geometry was converted into local scene meshes with its associated
textures. Original saved-state transforms were applied. Gazebo Classic material,
GUI and plugin settings were adapted for Gazebo Harmonic. The original unused
workcell model bundles are not included in this final distribution; the scene's
referenced geometry and texture files are retained.

Simulation additions
The primitive robots, routes, fires, particle textures, beacon network and
mission controllers extend the adapted warehouse scene. The existing Step 1
software and primitive robot were distributed under Apache-2.0. The final
packaging retains all applicable source notices and license texts.
